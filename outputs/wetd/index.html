What inference this adds

This section avoids obvious cause-effect claims. It shows the added inference: co-movement, theater lift, confidence limits, and data gaps that are not visible from any one raw source row.

1. Co-movement, not one obvious cause

Observed: United Arab Emirates (ARE) has the highest complete score at 76.6 with 5/5 signals present.

Inference added: this is not "sanctions exist, therefore risk." It is a queue item because policy/access control, procurement allocation, trade/stockpiling proxies, and energy/minerals rows co-occur in the same country-month.

Analyst check: inspect which signal is driving the score before treating the country as a strategic-allocation case.

2. Same score, different theater meaning

Observed: China (CHN) rises from base 76.2 to theater-adjusted 94.1 in Taiwan Strait.

Inference added: the same economic row has different strategic meaning when it sits inside a theater exposure graph. This separates "high score" from "high theater consequence."

Analyst check: validate exposure_weight and multiplier before escalating the theater note.

3. Confidence is part of the finding

Observed: score rows have confidence mix: high: 2, low: 20, medium: 58.

Inference added: a high score with low/fallback confidence means "review priority," not certainty. This prevents the dashboard from becoming a rice-plant/rice-harvest tautology.

Analyst check: compare source_row_refs and data-quality cards before using the score in a recommendation.

4. Data gap becomes a decision constraint

Observed: EIA Open Data: missing_key; UN Comtrade: missing_key.

Inference added: missing key-gated sources do not merely reduce polish; they bound what the dashboard is allowed to claim. Trade/energy conclusions stay provisional until high-resolution sources are attached.

Analyst check: treat fallback rows as hypothesis generators, not measured trade or energy truth.

The five War-Economy Transition signals

Each country score is built from these five interpretable signals. The signal cards state what is being tested before the country drilldown shows country-specific rows.

autarky

Self-reliance pressure

Is a country reducing dependence on open-market supply through subsidies, domestic substitution, or sovereignty framing?

Evidence used: Policy events, energy/mineral dependency rows, and strategic-sector keywords.

stockpiling

Strategic stockpiling

Is buying or inventory behavior becoming security-driven rather than ordinary commercial replenishment?

Evidence used: Trade proxy rows, Arctic/energy access rows, and abnormal accumulation candidates.

trade_rewiring

Route rewiring

Are flows moving through third countries, corridors, or indirect routes rather than direct market routes?

Evidence used: Trade proxy rows plus sanctions/access-control pressure that can explain rerouting.

capital_access_control

Permissioning and access control

Is market access being replaced by state permission, sanctions, screening, export controls, or compute/cloud restrictions?

Evidence used: Federal Register policy events, sanctions rows, and restricted-access evidence.

civilian_military_allocation

Civilian-to-military allocation

Are civilian technologies, compute, cloud, AI, or microelectronics entering defense/intelligence priority lanes?

Evidence used: USAspending procurement rows and defense-allocation keywords.

## What inference this adds

- Co-movement: a country enters the review queue when multiple independent signals appear together, not because one obvious causal fact exists.
- Theater lift: the same country-month row can mean more or less depending on Taiwan Strait, Middle East, or Arctic exposure.
- Confidence constraint: fallback or low-confidence rows create review priority, not certainty.
- Data gap as finding: missing key-gated sources bound what the dashboard is allowed to claim.

## Five War-Economy Transition signals

- Self-reliance pressure (`autarky`): Is a country reducing dependence on open-market supply through subsidies, domestic substitution, or sovereignty framing? Evidence used: Policy events, energy/mineral dependency rows, and strategic-sector keywords.
- Strategic stockpiling (`stockpiling`): Is buying or inventory behavior becoming security-driven rather than ordinary commercial replenishment? Evidence used: Trade proxy rows, Arctic/energy access rows, and abnormal accumulation candidates.
- Route rewiring (`trade_rewiring`): Are flows moving through third countries, corridors, or indirect routes rather than direct market routes? Evidence used: Trade proxy rows plus sanctions/access-control pressure that can explain rerouting.
- Permissioning and access control (`capital_access_control`): Is market access being replaced by state permission, sanctions, screening, export controls, or compute/cloud restrictions? Evidence used: Federal Register policy events, sanctions rows, and restricted-access evidence.
- Civilian-to-military allocation (`civilian_military_allocation`): Are civilian technologies, compute, cloud, AI, or microelectronics entering defense/intelligence priority lanes? Evidence used: USAspending procurement rows and defense-allocation keywords.
